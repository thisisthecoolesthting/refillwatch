## Where waste reduction actually pays off

Packaging reduction from refill and concentrate formats, plus swapping single-use paper products for reusable alternatives, tends to move the needle more than chasing "natural" or "eco" ingredient claims on cleaning products. If you're auditing your own household waste, track what actually fills your trash for a week before assuming you know your biggest category -- most people are surprised by how much comes from paper products and food waste rather than plastic packaging.

## A note on greenwashing

Terms like "eco-friendly," "natural," and "non-toxic" aren't regulated by the FDA or EPA, so they can appear on a label regardless of actual formulation. Third-party certifications (EPA Safer Choice, Cradle to Cradle) are a more reliable signal than marketing language alone when comparing cleaning products.
